How to use this tool

  1. Select or Upload Map: Choose from any of the 49 worlds, or drop any new map image.
  2. Position the Pins: Drag each Level node (1–10) onto the stone bumps along the path. Drag the 2 🎁 Gift Chests to stairs/rest spots.
  3. Pixel Precision: Yellow crosshairs mark the geometric center. Click a pin and press Arrow Keys to nudge.
  4. Full Viewport Scaling: The entire map is rendered edge-to-edge without horizontal clipping. Click Fit to auto-fit to window.
  5. Export to Game: Click 📋 Copy World JSON and paste it into app/src/main/assets/worlds_config.json!
